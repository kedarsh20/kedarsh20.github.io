Being a Toastmasters member for a while, makes me feel that my club is a like a family and as a part of this small family, I need be there when required. 

There was a Toastmaster role (person who is responsible to choose the theme of the meeting and lead the meeting) availabe and there was no volunteer. It was also a good opportunity for me to master my stage skills. When a Toastmasters meeting falls on May 4th, what can be a better theme than "May the 4th be with you". 

Though I had no idea about this phrase, I chose this theme as it was a perfect fit. When I did a little research, I came to know that May 4th is celebrated as a Star-Wars day (started since 2011). The phrase "May the force be with you" comes from Star-Wars films. That sounds similar to "May the 4th be with you" and this phrase was first time used in a newpaper by her party worker to express their support for the first female prime minister of UK "Margaret Thatcher". I decided to continue with this theme and decided to connect it with Star-Wars.

"Force" is the universal power that pushed humanbeings to face the challenges and do what to better for humankind.

<img src="/images/Toastmaster.png" width="200">

In the context of Toastmaster the force is "the power of Speaking": every word we speak has the power to 
- Inspire people
- Connect people
- Change their lives

I got inspired by the phrases and words used in Star-Wars and that can be connected well with Toastmasters. Here are the some words: 
Jedi council - Club executive council 
Yoda - Mentor
Padawan - Mentee

Some words and phrases can be used for different roles:
Timer- The clock keeper:  Don’t drift into deep space of thoughts while speaking
Word master- word wizard: Stay on target word "Word of the day"
Grammarian - language guardian: Follow the rule of the language
Stumble catcher - Avoid the use of junk of our speaking space ("Space junk")

Some phrases using the words:
Cut the filler word with your Light saber
When you see the red card you need to wind up your speech with a Light speed

These phrase can be used as TableTopics:
- "Do, or do not, there is no try": There should not hesitation of doing task that need to be done.
- "Your focus determines your reality": Good speaking is all about he focus on the listening at real time.
- "In my experience, there is no such thing as luck": Luck is noting without hard-work.
- "Failure is the greatest teacher"
- "You can’t stop the change, anymore than you can stop the sun from setting"
- "Always pass on what you have learned": Experienced members need to pass their experiences to their mentees or other members. 
- "This is where the fun begins": When Table-Topics session begins
- "Trust your instincts": Trust yourself, if you don't trust yourself noone will.
