Do you have a dream for a long time and you wanna achieve it?

 In 2017, I had a dream to complete a marathon. Before deciding to go for a marathon, I had successfully completed two half marathons. I was trying my best to complete this marathon but after 25KM, I started to get a stomach pain that made it difficult to run. To finish the marathon, the only choice I had was to walk and overcome the pain and start running again. But it didn’t help as soon as I started to run, the pain was stopping me. I had to stop the marathon in the middle and my dream was not fulfilled.

This year, on April 27, I participated in the Albi marathon and after 8 years, I successfully accomplished my dream. How was it possible? I made it possible by CAP. CAP: Consistency, Adaptation and Patience.

Consistency: At the end of 2017, I was getting more busy with my studies and career. Later to prepare for my PhD. I didn’t get enough time to train myself for a marathon. What I did during this time, I consistently maintained my fitness by exercising and running frequently about 5-10 Kms. 

Adaptation: In the last 8 years, I studied or worked at 4 different places that have different climates from tropical to dry cold. For example, when I came to Toulouse, during winter, I started my first running, I was hardly able to run for half a km and I started having chest pain due to breathing a cold air 4 degree celsius and I had to stop running. If I had to continue my fitness I had to adapt to this climate. Therefore, when it was very cold I started going to the gym for cardio and when the temperature was moderate I used to run outdoors. Slowly, I adapted myself to run even at below zero degrees.

Patience: Patience is the important key to finish the marathon successfully. This was the main reason I was not successful in completing the marathon 8 years ago. Running a marathon takes a few hours to finish and you have limited energy and stamina. If you run faster than the natural pace, you will be exhausted or may get some problem before the completion of the marathon.

Let me share a brief experience of my Albi marathon, it was the day I will never forget in my life. I generally start from the back of all the runners for two reasons: starting slowly and having to see all runners before me and the ambiance of cheering each other. That day the weather was so dynamic: sunny, rainy and cloudy. I took my natural pace patiently, enjoying the people cheering around me, enjoying the green valley along the river Tarn, and eating refreshments. My target was to finish around 4h but I finished it in 3h43m.

This is how CAP helped me achieve my long awaited dream successfully. I hope CAP may help you to achieve your dream that you planned. Nothing is more satisfying than achieving our dreams.
