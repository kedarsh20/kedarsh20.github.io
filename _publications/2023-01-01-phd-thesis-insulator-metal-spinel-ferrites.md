This thesis investigates the microscopic mechanisms responsible for insulator-to-metal transitions at interfaces between spinel ferrites (CoFe₂O₄, NiFe₂O₄) and ferroelectric perovskite oxides. Using density functional theory (DFT+U) and first-principles calculations on HPC clusters, the work explores how charge transfer, cation ordering, oxygen vacancies, and interfacial coupling collectively drive changes in the electronic and magnetic ground state of these heterostructures — with implications for spintronics and oxide electronics.
