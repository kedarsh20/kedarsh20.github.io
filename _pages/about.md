I am a computational physicist specializing in scientific modeling and simulation of complex materials. My work lies at the intersection of quantum mechanics, materials science, and high-performance computing, where I use density functional theory (DFT) to investigate electronic, magnetic, and structural phenomena at the atomic scale in complex oxide systems.

I recently completed a postdoctoral position at [CEMES-CNRS](https://www.cemes.fr), Toulouse, where I worked on thermodynamic modelling of molecules for energy storage as part of the European [ESiM project](https://www.esim-project.eu). Before that, my PhD research at the same institute focused on electronic transport at spinel ferrite/perovskite interfaces under the [MULTINANO ANR project](https://anr.fr), supervised by [Dr. Rémi Arras](mailto:remi.arras@cemes.fr).

## Research Interests

My research centres on understanding the electronic, magnetic, and structural properties of correlated oxide materials from first principles. Key topics include:

- **Spinel ferrites** (NiFe₂O₄, CoFe₂O₄) — cation ordering, defects, surfaces, and interfaces
- **Insulator-to-metal transitions** at oxide heterostructure interfaces
- **Oxygen vacancies and point defects** and their role in tuning electronic and magnetic properties
- **Thermodynamic simulations** of molecular systems for energy storage applications
- **Electronic transport** modelling using DFT-based approaches (Perturbo, BoltzTraP)
