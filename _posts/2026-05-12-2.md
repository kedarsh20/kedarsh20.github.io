“The mind is everything. What you think, you become.” — Gautama Buddha

In september 2014, yes quite a long time ago, during the first year of my graduation, I was sitting with some friends and one friend came to me and asked to participate for a relay race for our team in intra-college sport competition. I asked him which team, and I came to know that all the students were divided into different teams. Until that time, I never participated in any type of sports competition, so I was curious and I volunteered to participate in the relay. Guess which position my team got, third position, a bronze medal. By the way, there were only four teams. Not bad. I was surprised that my team's captain Alex came and encouraged me to join the athletic team and train for upcoming inter-college athletics, though I was not a good runner. As I was not doing any sports, this event encouraged me to become an athlete.

I started my training for a 5km and 10km race under the guidance of my Coach Sunny. Every morning he used to train me. He was getting training on different terrains such as on the tracks, on hills, along the roads, at the beach. My favourite training place was the beach, sometimes on the sand and sometimes on the water. My coach was like a mentor for me. He supervised my training schedule and studies, diets.  Within a month of training, I improved my pace a lot.
But this happiness didn't last longer. For one week, I got pain at latae muscles and it made it difficult to run. My coach told me to consult a doctor and the doctor told me that my body is not fit to run for competitions and I had to stop running completely to avoid further damage. That moment, I contemplated the future of my athletic career that was yet to begin. I was highly disappointed and talked about the doctor's response to my coach and he told me not to worry and one week completely rest and regular massage with hot and cold water for a week. I followed his instructions as he was the only hope for me to get rid of that trouble. 

After one week, I went for the training and found that the pain was almost gone but a bit of pain was there. It was a game of psychology. My fear that pain should not come was a daunting experience. Then my coach recommended that I go for yoga and meditation classes. That time, yoga and meditations were boring things for me but I had to follow my coach’s instructions and found that meditation changed my life completely. Controlling the focus of mind helps me to cure many injuries during my athletics career.

<img src="/images/IMG-20181221-WA0011.jpg" width="300">

This is my journey: how the inspiring and supporting people made me an athlete that made me win all the Gold medals in long distance running in all intercollege competitions. 

Therefore, Our life doesn’t stop giving us challenges. To overcome the challenges and succeed in life, we need to build or put ourselves in a positive environment surrounded with people who inspire you, support you, and guide you.
